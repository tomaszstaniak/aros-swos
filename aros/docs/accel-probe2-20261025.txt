OpenSWOS accel-probe2 (2026-10-25), AROS x86_64 ABIv11.
Experimental replacement executable for rc2 and accel-probe1.
Back up the installed SWOS; replace only SWOS in its existing directory.
Use Start for windowed or Start-Fullscreen with renderer=accel for fullscreen.
The explicit fullscreen command is:
SWOS --renderer=accel --fullscreen=desktop --scaling=sharp
Live accelerated fullscreen switching remains blocked by a requester.
